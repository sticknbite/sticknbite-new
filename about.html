Who We Are

Stick'n'Bite: Where Churrasco Meets San Diego

Stick'n'Bite was born in 2020 from a shared passion that Brazilians and Americans have always had in common: the love of a great barbecue. We took the soul of authentic Brazilian churrasco — the fire, the fresh-cut meats, the community gathered around the grill — and brought it to San Diego's events scene.

What started as a vision became something bigger: an experience. Not just catering, but a moment your guests step into and don't want to leave. We believe food is the center of every great gathering, and we take that seriously.

Based in San Diego, we serve weddings, corporate events, birthday parties, and private gatherings across Southern California — bringing the same level of passion and precision to every single event, whether it's 30 guests or 300.
