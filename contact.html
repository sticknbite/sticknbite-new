FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of events do you cater? +

We cater weddings, corporate events, birthday parties, quinceañeras, anniversaries, and all types of private gatherings across San Diego and Southern California.

What is the minimum guest count? +

Our minimum is 30 guests. We comfortably serve events from 30 up to 300 guests.

How far in advance should I book? +

We recommend booking 4–6 weeks in advance for most events. For weddings and large events, 3–6 months ahead is ideal since our calendar fills quickly — especially for summer weekends.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions? +

Absolutely. We accommodate gluten-free, vegetarian, and many other dietary needs. Our pão de queijo is naturally gluten-free. Just let us know your requirements when you request your quote and we'll build your menu accordingly.

Do you provide setup, service, and cleanup? +

Yes — all of it. Our team arrives early to set up completely, serves your guests throughout the event, and handles full cleanup at the end. You don't lift a finger.

Can you provide drinks for my event? +

Two ways, depending on what you need. For alcoholic drinks, we partner with a bar catering service and can connect you with a quote — just check the box on the form above and we'll include it in your proposal. If you'd rather skip the bar, we offer our own non-alcoholic Beverage Station — bottled water, soda, juice, iced tea, sparkling and infused water, including the setup, ice and cups.

What is your service area? +

We are based in San Diego and serve all of San Diego County and Southern California, including Los Angeles and surrounding areas.

What is your cancellation policy? +

Our cancellation policy details are included in your event contract. Please contact us directly at contact@sticknbite.com or (619) 793-7006 for specifics.
